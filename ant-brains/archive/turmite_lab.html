Turmite Wiring Lab

Langton's ant with extra rules. Each ant reads the colour under it and its own state, then looks up one row of the rule table: what colour to leave, which way to turn, which state to switch to, and whether to drop a neuron, split in two, or stop. Ants that walk from one neuron onto another lay a wire between them. The rule table is the genome.

Evolve the rules

Each generation makes 16 mutants of the current genome, grows each one for 3,000 steps off-screen, and keeps the best. Fitness rewards lots of neurons, about four wires each, many two-way wires, and roughly 15% long-range shortcuts: the sparse, small-world wiring a brain has.
